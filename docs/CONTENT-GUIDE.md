# Content guide

Rules for everything in `src/content` and the blog. The site is for **Texas Solutions RCM** (rcm.texassolutions.co), the healthcare revenue cycle management division of Texas Solutions, serving US medical and dental practices.

## Facts we may state
- Company: Texas Solutions, US office 401 W Kentucky Ave, Midland, TX 79701.
- Email info@texassolutions.co, phone +1 (838) 910-3147.
- Work is delivered by a remote team that works inside the client's own practice management, EHR and clearinghouse systems.
- Consultations are free.

## Things we may NOT state (until the owner approves them)
- Any statistic about our own results (collection increases, denial reductions, days in AR, number of clients, years in business).
- Prices, percentages of collections, turnaround times, response times or SLAs.
- Certifications (AAPC, AHIMA, HFMA), "HIPAA compliant", "HIPAA certified", "SOC 2", "100% accurate", "guaranteed".
- Client names, testimonials, case studies, partner logos, named software "integrations" or "partnerships".
- Claims that we have worked with a specific specialty before.

Say instead: "designed to", "aims to", "can help", "we follow", "we work inside your systems". When a practice needs a BAA, say we sign a Business Associate Agreement before accessing patient data (this is a standard industry requirement the owner has to honor anyway).

## Style
- American English, plain and specific. Short paragraphs. Second person ("your practice").
- No em dashes. No hype words: "revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "seamless", "unlock", "supercharge", "game-changer", "world-class", "leverage".
- Industry facts (how claims move, what CARC means, how Medicare enrollment works) must be accurate as of 2026 and general enough to stay true; name the authority when relevant (CMS, CAQH, NUCC, X12, ADA, AMA).
- CPT and CDT code descriptors are licensed (AMA, ADA). Do not reproduce descriptors. Mentioning a code number in passing is fine.
- FAQs answer the question in the first sentence.
- Every number in a calculator benchmark needs a real source URL and date. If unsure, leave benchmarks out.
